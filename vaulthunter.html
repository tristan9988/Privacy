Privacy Policy

Applies to the Android game Vault Hunter (package com.vaulthunter.game), published on Google Play by TristanEv.

Last updated: 26 November 2026

The short version. Vault Hunter has no accounts of its own and we do not collect, sell or receive your personal data. Your game progress is saved on your phone. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. Optional bonus videos are provided by Google AdMob, which collects some device information to show and measure ads, as explained below.

Your game progress

Your save (cash, gems, items, upgrades and settings) is stored only on your device. If you have Android's backup turned on, Android may back this save up to your own Google account so it can be restored on a new phone. We cannot see these backups. Uninstalling the game deletes the save from your device.

Cloud saves (Google Play Games)

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, a copy of your save is stored in your own Google account through Google Play Games Saved Games, so you can keep your progress on a new phone. Your Play Games name may be shown in the game. This data is held by Google, not by us; you can delete it from your Google account or the Play Games app at any time. See Google's privacy policy.

Purchases

Gem packs are sold through Google Play Billing. Your payment details are entered with Google and are never seen by, passed through or stored by Vault Hunter. The game only receives confirmation of which item you bought so it can be delivered. See Google's privacy policy.

Optional bonus videos (Google AdMob)

Vault Hunter never shows pop-up or banner ads. An ad only plays if you choose to watch a video for a bonus. These videos are served by Google AdMob, which may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information such as model, operating system, language, and crash or performance diagnostics;
  • how you interact with the ads.

Google uses this to deliver, measure and (where you allow it) personalise ads. In the UK, EEA and Switzerland the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Data we receive

None. Vault Hunter has no servers of its own and sends nothing to the developer. We do not sell or share personal data.

Children

Vault Hunter is intended for players aged 16 and over and is not directed at children.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. To remove your game data, uninstall the game. For data processed by Google, use the controls in your Google account or contact Google.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated, and material changes will be noted in the game's release notes.
